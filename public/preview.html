What's on the farm

Kubo and billiards

A full-size billiard table under a nipa roof, with a hammock strung beside it and the fields on every side.

Bonfire

A stone fire pit ringed with carved hardwood benches. The caretaker lights it and puts it out — ₱250 covers the wood.

Half court

A full concrete half court on the grounds.

Two casitas, one group

Both A-frames are yours — air-conditioned, lit up at night, and mirrored in the pool between them.

Tent pitching

Shaded ground to pitch tents if your group runs long.

Farm views

Open highland field and tree line on every side.

What this page is

A look at the design and content, in one file with the photos built in — it opens anywhere, with nothing running.

The working site is more than this. Live availability, the farm map that shows which unit is free on a date, the booking form with its running price, receipt upload, the admin panel and the Airbnb sync all need the server and the database. Those run at http://localhost:3007 once npm run db:dev and npm run dev are going.
